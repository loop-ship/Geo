Privacy Policy

Pole-Shift Simulator — website (poleshift.app) and the iOS and Android apps. Last updated 20 October 2026.

Short version: the apps collect no data at all. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere. The website counts anonymous page views without cookies.

The iOS and Android apps

  • The simulator runs entirely on your device. The globe, terrain and physics are bundled inside the app, and it works without an internet connection.
  • The apps contain no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and make no network requests of their own.
  • The app stores a few display preferences on your device only (for example, whether you have already seen the introduction panel). These never leave the device and are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • If you use Share, the app hands a link describing your current view (pole position and settings — nothing personal) to your device's own share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you.
  • Tapping a link to this page or another website opens it in your browser, which has its own privacy terms.

The website

  • The website uses GoatCounter to count page views and a few anonymous events (such as "app installed"). GoatCounter sets no cookies and does not build profiles or track you across sites. It is skipped entirely if your browser sends Do Not Track.
  • Like any website, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) receives standard request information such as your IP address in order to deliver the page. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The 3D libraries are loaded from the jsDelivr CDN, which likewise receives standard request information.
  • The optional tip jar on the website links to Ko-fi; nothing is shared with Ko-fi unless you choose to visit it.

Children

The simulator is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/loop-ship/Geo/issues.
