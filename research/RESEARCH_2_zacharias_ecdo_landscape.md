# The Zacharias Pole-Shift Model (@zachariaspro / GEOSYNC): What It Is and How It Differs from ECDO, Hapgood, and Sitchin

## TL;DR
- The "Zacharias model" is the work of an X/Twitter poster known as **Zacharias (@zachariaspro)**, who runs **geosyncmonitor.com** ("GEOSYNC // Earth Orientation Monitor") — a data-driven tool that ingests official IERS Earth-orientation data and argues that Earth's **Chandler Wobble is collapsing**, signaling the **onset of True Polar Wander (TPW)**, i.e., a near-90° geographic pole shift. It is observationally *aligned with* The Ethical Skeptic's ECDO theory but is a separate, independently-derived, geodetic/observational framework that places the new pole in a *different* spot from ECDO.
- The single most important distinction: **ECDO claims a specific physical cause** (exothermic core-mantle decoupling → a gyroscopic "Dzhanibekov" flip of ~104° along the 31°E meridian, ending near ~14°S/31°E in Zambia). **The Zacharias model is primarily observational** — it tracks the live rotation/wobble signal and simulates the *geometry and consequences* of a hard swing, and points to a different new-north-pole location (off equatorial West Africa / the Gulf of Guinea, a ~90–91° swing). **Hapgood** (slow crustal slippage ≤40°, ice-driven) and **Sitchin** (external Nibiru gravity) are fundamentally different frameworks.
- Hard evidence on the model's internal details (the "five papers," the simulator's Tier system, exact pole coordinates, sea-level numbers) is **thinly documented in public/indexable sources**, because the website is a JavaScript app and X posts are not directly retrievable. Treat the specific numbers as model outputs, not verified science.

## Key Findings

**1. Who is @zachariaspro (Zacharias)?** An independent researcher/analyst on X, listed on David Sunfellow's "The Coming Pole Shift" resource page with website geosyncmonitor.com. Real-world identity and credentials are not disclosed. His site presents the "GEOSYNC // Earth Orientation Monitor," self-described as "OBSERVATIONAL · IERS FINALS2000A · ENGINE v3.0 · PROVENANCE-TAGGED," with data states "LIVE = daily IERS," "STATIC = published," "LEGACY = frozen." The reportedly "five papers" could not be fully enumerated; two confirmed titles: "The Death of Wobble and the Onset of True Polar Wander" and "The Wobble Collapse Is Real…". He appeared in a recorded discussion with The Ethical Skeptic (@EthicalSkeptic) and Craig Stone (@nobulart), and debated Craig Stone over polar-motion phase stability.

**2. Core mechanism — and how it differs from ECDO, Hapgood, Sitchin.**
- *Zacharias — observational/geodetic.* Argues from IERS polar-motion and length-of-day data that the **Chandler Wobble** (period ~433 days, surface amplitude ~9 m) is collapsing in amplitude, interpreted as a precursor to a large rapid TPW. (Not purely fringe: Wikipedia's "Chandler wobble" article notes "measurements indicate the Chandler wobble has greatly diminished" post-2015.) Emphasis on tracking the *live* signal and **simulating the resulting pole path and flooding geometry**, rather than committing to a single physical cause. This is the methodological core: it overlaps ECDO's *conclusion* (TPW) without adopting its *causal story*.
- *ECDO (The Ethical Skeptic).* Periodic exothermic phase changes in the nickel-iron core shed heat and lubricating D″ material; the core "loses its magnetic moment and decouples from the outer rotational body." A shift "along the 31st East meridian … moving towards South Africa" to "a 104-degree rotated momentum-conserving gyroscopic one (via a mediated Dzhanibekov TPW rotation)." New north pole ~14°S/31°E (eastern Zambia). Cyclic, internally driven, tied to the geomagnetic cycle; explicitly TPW "and not a 'tilt'."
- *Hapgood (Earth Crustal Displacement).* Shifts ≤40° about every 5,000 years; the *crust* slides while the spin axis stays fixed. Early driver was polar ice mass; in *The Path of the Pole* he conceded ice is insufficient and argued for subsurface causes.
- *Sitchin (Nibiru / Planet X).* A hypothetical planet on a long elliptical orbit whose periodic close passes disturb Earth. Ancient-astronaut/mythological framework, no geodetic basis, unrelated to the wobble/TPW data approach.

**Verdict on the user's question:** The user's belief is essentially correct. The Zacharias model is *related to and observationally aligned with* ECDO (same end-state — a ~90°+ TPW, not a mere axial tilt) but is **methodologically distinct**: built on live Earth-orientation data and forward simulation rather than ECDO's core-phase-change causal narrative, and predicts a different new-pole location. It shares neither Hapgood's ice-driven crustal-slip mechanism nor Sitchin's external-body cause. **There is no real Zacharias–Nibiru/Sitchin lineage** — that connection in the first research pass was a name-conflation (Zacharias vs Zecharia Sitchin), flagged for verification and here corrected.

## Details and corroborating evidence
- ECDO's defining claim: a shift "along the 31st East meridian … moving towards South Africa … to a 104-degree rotated momentum-conserving gyroscopic one (via a mediated Dzhanibekov TPW rotation)"; new north pole ~14°S/31°E (Zambia), new south pole ~14°N/149°W (central Pacific).
- The "wobble collapse" claim has a genuine observational kernel but is not unprecedented: Malkin & Miller (2010), "Chandler wobble: two more large phase jumps revealed," *Earth, Planets and Space*, found deep minima ~1850, ~1925, ~2005, each with a large phase jump. Mainstream counterpoint: amplitude collapses with phase jumps have recurred without triggering a cataclysmic pole shift.
- Mainstream geophysics rejects rapid cataclysmic pole shift: TPW rates "1° per million years or slower." Jurdy & Van der Voo (1975, *Science* 187:1193) found TPW "no larger than a few degrees." Current literature (*Proc. R. Soc. A* 481(2312):20240196, 2025) still frames the question as whether Earth has been stable (<5° TPW) or mobile (≫5°) since the Cretaceous — i.e. large/rapid versions remain outside mainstream support.

## Caveats
- Sourcing is overwhelmingly the proponents' own material (X/@zachariaspro, geosyncmonitor.com, sunfellow.com, theethicalskeptic.com, nobulart.com). No independent scientific analysis of the *Zacharias* model specifically appears to exist.
- The website and X posts could not be directly retrieved (JS app; X robots-blocked). Several specifics rest on the user's screenshot and secondary aggregation.
- "HashingZap" / @HashZappa posts simulations based on the Zacharias model; its outputs are illustrations, not independent corroboration.
- Mainstream geophysics classifies cataclysmic/rapid pole shift as pseudoscientific. The Zacharias, ECDO, Hapgood, and Sitchin frameworks are all outside accepted science; this report describes what each claims and how they differ, not an endorsement.
