## Elevator paragraph (About-page / press bio length)

Pole-Shift Simulator is a free, browser-based 3D globe that lets you pick a new location for
Earth's North Pole and watch a physically-grounded model play out the consequences in real time:
ocean redistribution, new coastlines, earthquake-prone zones, volcanic activity, and more. Its
defining feature is honesty — every effect is graded by scientific standing (established / debated
/ unverified) right on screen, with named sources. It runs the internet's most-cited "pole shift"
scenarios (Hapgood, ECDO, GEOSYNC, Davidson) faithfully and tells you exactly where each one
departs from mainstream geophysics — including one whose core mechanism is flatly impossible (a
micronova requires a companion white dwarf; the Sun has none), stated on screen, not buried in a
footnote. No signup, no cost, installable as an app if you want it. It's a "what-if" explorer, not
a forecast.

---

## Why it's different (the angle worth leading with)

Most "pole shift" content online is either credulous (repeats the claim) or dismissive (mocks it
and moves on). This tool does neither — it's cause-agnostic: you set the pole, it runs the real
kinematics, and it grades the result honestly. The most quotable proof point: the app's own
monument-alignment check shows the Great Pyramid matches *today's* pole to 0.067°, a **tighter**
match than the fringe pole it's supposedly evidence for — and it flags the "match" to that fringe
pole as a same-meridian coincidence, not corroboration. That's the app arguing against its own
premise with its own data, on screen, in real time.

## Key features (bullet list — use whichever subset fits)

- Set any point on Earth as the new North Pole; watch oceans redistribute in real time (not a
  cartoon — an actual physics model: Coulomb-stress proxy, real ETOPO bathymetry).
- Four widely-cited real-world scenarios pre-loaded: Hapgood (1958/70), ECDO (Roger B. Cunningham),
  GEOSYNC, and Davidson/Suspicious0bservers — each graded on-screen for scientific standing.
- Earthquake and volcano consequence layers, glacial-rebound seismicity, tsunami run-up estimates,
  climate-belt shifts — all cause-agnostic, all driven by wherever you set the pole.
- Monument-alignment checker: tests whether cited ancient-monument azimuths (Giza, Göbekli Tepe,
  Teotihuacán) actually support a claimed pole position, math shown on screen.
- "Play the shift" — watch the flood arrive at a readable pace instead of an instant jump-cut.
- Share any configured view with one link — the exact scenario reopens for whoever you send it to.
- Simple mode by default (four panels, plain language) with a full Advanced mode one tap away for
  every control power users want.
- Installable as an offline-capable PWA. Free. No account, no paywall, no ads.
